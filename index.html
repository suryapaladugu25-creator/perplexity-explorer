PANEL 01: THE TOKEN CIPHER

"What exactly is a token, Explorer?"
🧑‍🚀
"A piece of text that the model processes! Words, subwords, or even punctuation marks like commas!"
🤖
"Spot on! GPT-2 splits language into bite-sized subword tokens using Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE), mapping each to an ID from 0 to 50,256."
PANEL 02: THE SOFTMAX ARENA
🤖
"At every single word step, how does the model decide what comes next?"
🧑‍🚀
"The neural net generates raw scores called logits for all 50,257 vocabulary words, then Softmax turns them into probabilities that sum to 100%!"
🤖
"Exactly! A probability of 0.25 means the model assigns a 25% chance (or 1 in 4 odds) to that specific word."
PANEL 03: SURPRISE & CROSS-ENTROPY
🤖
"Surprise alert! Why do rare words blow up the model's loss?"
🧑‍🚀
"Because Information Surprise equals -log₂(P) bits! If a word had only a 1% probability (P=0.01), its surprise is 6.64 bits. If P=0.99, surprise is almost 0!"
🤖
"Brilliant! Cross-entropy loss is simply the average surprise across all words in your sentence."
PANEL 04: THE PERPLEXITY METRIC
🤖
"And now the grand finale: What does Perplexity actually mean in plain English?"
🧑‍🚀
"It's the effective branching factor! If Perplexity is 15, the model was on average as uncertain as rolling a 15-sided die at each step!"
🤖
"You've mastered the theory! Lower perplexity means natural, fluent flow. High perplexity means unusual or chaotic phrasing!"
